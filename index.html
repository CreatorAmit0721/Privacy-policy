Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Salon Booking Assistant ("the Service"), operated by Amit Kumar on behalf of Jawed Habib Salon, collects and uses information when you message the salon on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook.

Information we collect

  • Your name and phone number (or messaging ID).
  • Your messages to the salon, including service, date and time requests.
  • Appointment details (service, date, time, status).

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and book, change or cancel appointments.
  • To send appointment reminders and, where you have booked with us, occasional follow-up messages.
  • To let salon staff manage bookings.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is processed only by the tools needed to run the Service (Meta WhatsApp/Instagram/Facebook messaging, our automation platform n8n, and an AI language model used to understand and answer your messages). Data may be shared with salon staff or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep appointment records only as long as needed to run the salon's bookings. You can ask us to delete your data at any time, see Data Deletion Instructions.

Security

We use access-controlled systems and do not store passwords or payment card details in chats.
